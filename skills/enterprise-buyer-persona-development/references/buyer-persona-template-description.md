# Buyer persona template, full description

This describes the two-slide Buyer persona template from the Microsoft AI Product Manager Professional Certificate closely enough to rebuild it from scratch (for example with pptxgenjs) or to recognise and fill a copy the person attaches. Sizes are in inches, colors are hex RGB. Slide size is 13.33 x 7.5 (16:9), blank layout, both slides.

## 1. Slide 1: one persona

White background. An avatar at the top-left and seven flat rectangles (square corners, thin dark navy outline). Each rectangle has a centered heading at the top (sans-serif, about 14 pt, black) and, inside it, a left-aligned hint text box (12 pt, black, 2.1 x 0.9 in).

**Avatar:** a 1.69 x 1.69 picture at (0.62, 0.43): an illustrated woman with long auburn hair, navy blazer and orange top, inside a white circle, on a square warm gradient (peach and orange at the top-left to coral and pink at the bottom-right).

## 2. Slide 2: three personas side by side

**Background:** one full-bleed picture (13.33 x 7.5), a soft blurred gradient: steel blue in the top-left corner fading to pale teal and mint across the middle, with lavender-blue in the bottom-right corner. The row labels and the rules are baked into this picture, so they are not editable text in the original.

**Row labels** (uppercase, a light rounded sans-serif, dark grey, centered in a left label column about 2.2 in wide, centered near x = 2.7): `NAME` (y about 3.0), `GOALS` (about 3.9), `MOTIVATIONS` (about 4.7), `FRUSTRATIONS` (about 5.5), `QUOTABLE QUOTE` (about 6.3).

**Rules:** one thin black horizontal line under each row, from x about 1.14 to 12.7, at y about 3.45, 4.28, 5.10, 5.93 and 6.75.

**Avatars:** three rounded-square pictures (about 1.6 x 1.6) in a row at the top, at x about 4.4, 7.2 and 10.05 and y about 0.6: pale sky-blue with a white cloud and green rolling hills (placeholder art).

**Text slots:** three column groups (one per persona), each with five "Insert text" boxes (Arial 10, black, left-aligned, about 0.85 x 0.27) sitting just above each rule, one per row (`NAME`, `GOALS`, `MOTIVATIONS`, `FRUSTRATIONS`, `QUOTABLE QUOTE`). Column text starts at x about 4.8, 7.7 and 10.5.

## 3. How to rebuild it

1. Make a 13.33 x 7.5 deck with a blank layout.
2. Slide 1: draw the seven rectangles from section 1 (fill, thin navy outline, centered heading), add the hint text boxes, and place an avatar (a simple circle with the persona's initials is fine, since the original illustration is not reproduced).
3. Slide 2: use a soft gradient image as the background (steel blue top-left, pale teal middle, lavender-blue bottom-right), add the five row labels as real text on the left, the five black rules, three avatar placeholders, and the three columns of five text boxes.
4. Render both slides to images and compare them with this description.

## 4. How to fill it

- Slide 1: duplicate it once per persona (three slides). Replace each hint with that persona's content for the matching heading: Persona (name, age, role, department), Company Profile (company details, size, industry, global presence), Areas of Interest, Goals, Responsibilities, Purchasing Power (decision makers circle and budget), Pain Points. Keep headings as they are.
- The hint boxes are small (2.1 x 0.9). Enlarge each to the inside of its rectangle (about 0.2 in margin) and use 10 to 11 pt text, short bullets, so the content fits.
- Slide 2 goes last. Per persona column fill `NAME`, `GOALS`, `MOTIVATIONS`, `FRUSTRATIONS`, `QUOTABLE QUOTE` with one short line each. Enlarge the text boxes to the column width (about 2.4 in) if text is longer than a few words, and keep them above their rule.
- Mark invented details (age, interests, quotes) as illustrative, as the skill's main instructions require.
